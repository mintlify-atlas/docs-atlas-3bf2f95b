Every account is assigned a plan that controls monthly scan and resolution limits. All plans share the same scanner capabilities — only the quota limits differ.

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Monthly scans | Monthly resolutions |
|---|---|---|
| `starter` | 2 | 2 |
| `team` | 500 | 500 |
| `enterprise` | 2,000 | 2,000 |

New users are automatically placed on the **Starter** plan. No credit card is required to get started.

<Tip>
  Check your current plan and quota limits at any time with `GET /api/v1/user/profile`.

  ```bash
  curl https://code-security-platform.onrender.com/api/v1/user/profile \
       -H "Authorization: Bearer <JWT_TOKEN>"
  ```
</Tip>

## Upgrading your plan

Use the subscription endpoints below to move to a higher tier. Upgrading is additive — your new plan quota is added on top of any remaining quota from your current billing period, and your monthly counters are reset.

### Upgrade to Team

```bash
curl -X POST https://code-security-platform.onrender.com/api/v1/user/subscription/team \
     -H "Authorization: Bearer <JWT_TOKEN>"
```

```json Response
{
  "status": "success",
  "message": "User user@example.com successfully subscribed to TEAM plan."
}
```

### Upgrade to Enterprise

```bash
curl -X POST https://code-security-platform.onrender.com/api/v1/user/subscription/enterprise \
     -H "Authorization: Bearer <JWT_TOKEN>"
```

```json Response
{
  "status": "success",
  "message": "User user@example.com successfully subscribed to ENTERPRISE plan."
}
```

<Note>
  Downgrades are not supported through the API. Contact support to move to a lower tier.
</Note>
